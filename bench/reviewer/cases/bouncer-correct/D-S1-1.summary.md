## Summary
Wrote derivations/D-S1-1.tex and derivations/D-S1-1.summary.md. WKB quantisation in the allowed region between the floor and the classical turning point, matching the floor condition to the connection formula at the turning point.

## Result
For $V = mgz$ above a hard floor at $z = 0$, the WKB energy levels are $E_n = (\hbar^2 m g^2/2)^{1/3}\,[\tfrac{3\pi}{2}(n - \tfrac14)]^{2/3}$, $n = 1, 2, \dots$, from the quantisation condition $\int_0^{z_E} p\,dz = (n - \tfrac14)\pi\hbar$.

# Task
Find the energy levels of a particle of mass $m$ bouncing on a hard floor in uniform gravity, in the WKB approximation.

## Key steps
- Allowed region $0 < z < z_E$ (2), local momentum (3) and WKB phase (4).
- The floor condition gives the form (5); the connection formula at the turning point gives (6).
- Requiring (5) and (6) to agree gives the quantisation condition (7).
- The action integral (8) then gives (9), the levels (10) and the classical heights (11).

## Assumptions
- One-dimensional motion; the floor is perfectly rigid ($V = +\infty$ for $z \le 0$).
- WKB approximation in the allowed region, with the standard connection formula at the linear turning point $z_E$.

## Sanity checks
- Dimensions: $(\hbar^2 m g^2)^{1/3}$ is an energy, and $\ell_g = (\hbar^2/2m^2g)^{1/3}$ a length.
- Large $n$: $E_n \propto n^{2/3}$, as required by the classical phase-space area $\oint p\,dz \propto E^{3/2}$.
- The levels are positive and increase with $n$, and the heights (11) satisfy $z_n = E_n/(mg)$.
