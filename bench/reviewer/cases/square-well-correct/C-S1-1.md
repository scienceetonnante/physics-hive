# Task
Compute the second virial coefficient $B_2(T)$ of a gas of molecules interacting through the square-well potential, in closed form.

## Computation
$B_2 = -2\pi\int_0^\infty f(r)\,r^2\,dr$ with the Mayer function $f = e^{-u/kT} - 1$.
- Symbolic path: SymPy integrates $f\,r^2$ piecewise over the core ($f = -1$) and the well; beyond $\lambda\sigma$, $f = 0$. The result is compared with the closed form above.
- Numerical path: SciPy `quad` integrates the Mayer function over the core and the well separately, for $\sigma = \epsilon = 1$, $\lambda = 1.5$, at $kT/\epsilon = 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 20, 100$.

## Results
$$B_2 = \frac{2 \pi \sigma^{3}}{3}\left[1 - \left(\lambda^{3} - 1\right) \left(e^{\epsilon/kT} - 1\right)\right]$$
SymPy confirms that its integral minus the closed form simplifies to 0.

## Sanity checks
- High temperature: $B_2 \to b_0 = 2\pi\sigma^3/3$, the hard-sphere value (checked with `sympy.limit`).
- No well ($\lambda = 1$): $B_2 = b_0$ at every temperature.
- Dimensions: $B_2$ is $\sigma^3$ times a dimensionless function of $\epsilon/kT$ and $\lambda$, a volume per molecule.
