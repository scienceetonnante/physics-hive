# Brief for C-S1-1

## Target

For the simple pendulum defined in `conventions.md` (point mass on a massless rigid rod of length L, uniform gravity g, no friction), compute the coefficient a in

  T(θ₀) = T₀ (1 + a θ₀² + O(θ₀⁴)),   T₀ = 2π√(L/g),

where T(θ₀) is the exact period at amplitude θ₀ (in radians). Work without units in ω₀ = 1, so T₀ = 2π.

1. **Symbolic.** Starting from an exact expression of the period (state it and say where it comes from), obtain the series of T/T₀ in θ₀ with SymPy and read off a as an exact rational. Report the θ₀⁴ coefficient too if the series gives it directly.
2. **Numerical, two independent paths.**
   - Evaluate the exact period through the complete elliptic integral, with mpmath at high precision (and SciPy for comparison), at several amplitudes spanning roughly 1e-3 to 1 rad. Mind the modulus/parameter convention stated in `conventions.md`.
   - Measure the period independently of any elliptic-integral formula: integrate the equation of motion θ'' + sin θ = 0 numerically from rest at θ₀ (tight tolerances) and time the oscillation, at a subset of the same amplitudes. Compare with the elliptic values.
3. **Extract a from the numbers.** Tabulate (T/T₀ − 1)/θ₀² against θ₀ and show its limit as θ₀ → 0. Also fit T/T₀ − 1 at small amplitudes with a polynomial in θ₀² (include higher-order terms as needed) and report the fitted a with an error estimate. Compare with the symbolic value.
4. **Accuracy of the truncated formula.** Tabulate the relative error of T₀(1 + a θ₀²) against the exact period at amplitudes 0.1, 0.2, 0.5, 1.0 rad.
