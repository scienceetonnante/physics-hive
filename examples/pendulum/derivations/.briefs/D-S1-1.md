# Brief for D-S1-1

## Target

For the simple pendulum (point mass on a massless rigid rod of length $L$, no friction, uniform gravity $g$), derive analytically the period $T(\theta_0)$ as a function of the amplitude $\theta_0$, to first non-trivial order in $\theta_0$: find the coefficient $c_2$ in

$$T(\theta_0) = T_0\,\bigl(1 + c_2\,\theta_0^2 + O(\theta_0^4)\bigr), \qquad T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{L/g}.$$

State $c_2$ as an exact number. State also the exact expression of the period from which the expansion is taken, if your derivation goes through one.
