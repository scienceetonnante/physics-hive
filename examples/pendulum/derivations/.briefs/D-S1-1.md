# Brief for D-S1-1

## Target

For the simple pendulum defined in `conventions.md` (point mass on a massless rigid rod of length L, uniform gravity g, no friction), derive analytically the coefficient a in

  T(θ₀) = T₀ (1 + a θ₀² + O(θ₀⁴)),   T₀ = 2π√(L/g),

where T(θ₀) is the exact period at amplitude θ₀ (in radians). Give a as an exact rational number.

Obtain a by two independent analytic routes inside the same derivation if feasible, and say whether they agree. State every step: where the period comes from, which expansion is made, and why the neglected terms are O(θ₀⁴).
