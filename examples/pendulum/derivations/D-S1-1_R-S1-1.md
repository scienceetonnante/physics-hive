## Summary

## Result
I re-derived both routes symbolically with SymPy and checked the result numerically against a direct integration of the equation of motion that uses neither route. Route 1 checks out: the series 2K/π = 1 + m/4 + 9m²/64 + …, the Wallis coefficients c_n for n ≤ 4, the expansion of k² and k⁴ in θ₀, and the composed series T/T₀ = 1 + θ₀²/16 + 11θ₀⁴/3072 + 173θ₀⁶/737280 + …. Route 2 checks out as well: the forcing (λ + 1/8)cos τ + (1/24)cos 3τ, the value λ = −1/8, and u₃ = (cos τ − cos 3τ)/192, which satisfies its equation and initial conditions; the residual of the equation of motion vanishes through order ε³. I integrated θ'' = −sin θ from rest with mpmath's ODE solver, which uses neither elliptic integrals nor perturbation theory. Its period agrees with (2/π)T₀K(sin(θ₀/2)) to 15 digits for θ₀ from 0.05 to 1.0. The ratio (T/T₀ − 1)/θ₀² goes to 0.0625 as θ₀ → 0, and the next-order residual is consistent with a θ₀⁴ term and no θ₀³ term. The tests on dimensions, limits and parity pass, and the hand evaluation at θ₀ = 0.5 reproduces to all quoted digits. My remarks are all cosmetic, so none affects the claim.

## Flags
- The by-product coefficient 11/3072 of θ₀⁴ (Eq. 15) is also confirmed by my checks: the SymPy series gives it exactly, and the ODE residual (T/T₀ − 1 − θ₀²/16)/θ₀⁴ tends to 0.003581 ≈ 11/3072. It was not the claim under review, so this review should not be cited as the main evidence for it if a card is written.

## Remarks
- [cosmetic] Section 3, line 172 against lines 187–189: the text says Route 2 "does not use the energy integral", but identifying the amplitude with ε does invoke energy conservation. This does not affect the result, because u(0) = ε with u'(0) = 0 makes ε a turning point directly. The claim that the two routes "share only Eq. (1)" is slightly overstated.
- [cosmetic] Line 193: the phrase "amplitude −ε" is loose, since the amplitude is defined as positive. The intended statement is that the frequency for the initial displacement −ε equals the frequency for +ε, so ω is even in the initial displacement. The argument is correct.
- [cosmetic] Lines 280–281: the sign argument (sin θ < θ, so the restoring torque is weaker and the swings are slower) is heuristic. It is offered only as a consistency check, and both routes give a > 0 anyway.

## Verdict
Confirmed. Both analytic routes of D-S1-1 are correct step by step. I reproduced them symbolically, and both give a = 1/16. The O(θ₀⁴) remainder is properly justified by Route 1, through the bound of Eq. 11 and the analyticity and evenness of (2/π)K(sin(θ₀/2)). A direct numerical integration of the equation of motion, independent of both routes, reproduces the exact period and the limit (T/T₀ − 1)/θ₀² → 1/16. The only remarks are cosmetic, and none affects the claim.
