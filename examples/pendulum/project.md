# What is the period of a pendulum to second order in the amplitude?

## Goal

Find the period T of the simple pendulum (point mass on a massless rigid rod, no friction, uniform gravity) to first non-trivial order in the amplitude θ₀,
that is the coefficient of the θ₀² correction to the small-oscillation period
2π√(L/g). An answer is this coefficient, derived analytically and checked
against a numerical evaluation of the exact period (complete elliptic integral)
for several amplitudes.
